Classic Skyjo · Clear, then 3

  1. Take a complete column. If the available card makes three equal numbers vertically, take the clear.
  2. Keep 3 or lower. Replace your highest visible 4 or higher. If you have none, put the low card in a hidden slot.
  3. Pass on the rest. If the discard does neither, draw blind. Apply the same two rules. If you pass on the drawn card, flip a hidden slot.

For the tested tie-break, use the leftmost column, then the topmost eligible card. Your family can play without tracking other players or counting cards.

Remember the finish. Revealing your last hidden card gives everyone else one final turn. If another player ties or beats your positive round score, yours doubles.
