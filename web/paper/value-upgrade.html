Clear or trade?

Classic Skyjo · Start with the top discard. If you draw blind, use the steps again with the card you drew.

  1. Find your best ordinary trade. Set aside any spot that would make three equal cards in a vertical column. A visible card qualifies if it is 4 or higher and the incoming card is 3 or lower. After a blind draw, any visible card also qualifies if the swap saves at least 4 points. Replace your highest qualifying card. If none qualifies, a 3 or lower can go in a hidden spot.
  2. Compare the savings. Count each hidden card as 5. Clearing saves the sum of the three cards now in the column. Your ordinary trade saves the old card minus the incoming card. Passing saves 0. Choose the best clear if it ties or beats your ordinary trade (or 0 if you have none).
  3. Make your move. Otherwise make the ordinary trade. If neither move is worth taking, pass on the discard and draw blind. If you already drew, discard it and flip a hidden card. For tied targets, use the leftmost column, then the topmost spot.

Example. With a 1 available, clearing a column of 1, 1 and a hidden card saves about 7. Replacing a 12 with that 1 saves 11, so take the trade.
